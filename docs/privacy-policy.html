Privacy Policy for StrengthForge

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StrengthForge ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the StrengthForge Android application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information (via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication): When you sign in with Google, we receive your Firebase User ID (UID), email address, and basic profile display name solely to authenticate you and keep your workout data private to your account.
  • Fitness & Workout Data You Enter: Workout session names, dates, durations, notes, exercise definitions, sets, weights, repetitions, timed-hold durations, and custom routines stored in your private Cloud Firestore workspace.
  • Nutrition & Macro Logs You Enter: Daily calorie and macronutrient (protein, carbs, fats) targets, meal logs, and custom meal presets.
  • Optional Google Sheets Sync Data: If you explicitly choose to connect Google Sheets, the App requests Google OAuth permission (limited to creating and updating the spreadsheet you create with StrengthForge) to write your workout and nutrition summaries directly to your Google Drive. We do not read or access your unrelated Google Drive files.
  • Crash Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics): We collect anonymized crash stack traces and basic non-identifying device state (such as selected unit preference or app version) via Firebase Crashlytics strictly to diagnose and fix app stability issues. We do not attach names, emails, or personal workout logs to crash reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core app functionality: workout logging, progression charts, 1RM estimates, and macro tracking.
  • Sync and back up your records across devices using Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.
  • Generate user-requested exports (local CSV files, Garmin .FIT files, workout share graphics, or Google Sheets sync).
  • Respond to support or account deletion requests sent to mbrothen@gmail.com.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Sharing

StrengthForge does not sell your personal data, does not include third-party ad networks, and does not share your workout or nutrition records with data brokers.

We rely strictly on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Crashlytics) to store and stabilize your app experience under encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections, and optionally the Google Sheets / Drive API only when you initiate a spreadsheet sync.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your workout and nutrition data is retained only as long as your account remains active. You may export your data at any time in CSV format or permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly inside the app or via our Account & Data Deletion page.

5. Security

All network communication with Firebase and Google APIs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Firestore Security Rules restrict read and write access so only your authenticated account can access your user records.

6. Children's Privacy

StrengthForge is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by law in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Health & Fitness Disclaimer

StrengthForge is an informational workout and nutrition logging tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning any exercise or nutrition program.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: mbrothen@gmail.com.
