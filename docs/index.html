Core Features

Progressive Overload & PR Tracking

Automatic starting weight, current max, estimated 1RM (Epley), last-session reference, and live "NEW PR!" badges while you train.

Interactive Strength Analytics

Timeframe filtering (This Week, Last 30 Days, All Time), interactive progression curves, volume milestones, and muscle group balance charts.

Built-In Gym Tools

Barbell plate calculator, customizable rest countdown timer with audible beep alerts, and live timed-hold timers for planks and isometric work.

Macro & Meal Logging

Track daily calories, protein, carbs, and fats with multi-day history, reusable meal presets, custom targets, and daily reset/edit controls.

Full Data Portability

Export workouts and nutrition to local CSV spreadsheets, export Garmin Connect .FIT files, or optionally sync directly to your own Google Sheets workbook.

Cloud Sync Across Devices

Secured with Google Sign-In and private per-user Firebase Cloud Firestore storage so your logs stay backed up when switching phones.
