Core Features

Progressive Overload & Social PR Sharing

Automatic starting weight, current max, estimated 1RM (Epley), live “NEW PR!” badges, and one-tap shareable PR and Workout Summary graphics right from the Dashboard.

Global lbs / kg & Plate Calculator

Switch between Imperial (45 lb bar) and Metric (20 kg Olympic barbell with 25, 20, 15, 10, 5, 2.5, and 1.25 kg plates) across workouts, analytics, and exports.

Goal-Based Onboarding & Custom Week Start

Start with Cut, Maintain, or Bulk targets plus Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower, or Full Body schedules—and pick Sunday, Monday, or any day to start your training week.

Lock-Screen Rest & Timed-Hold Alerts

Customizable default rest durations, live timed-hold timers, audible beep cues, and optional background local notifications with sound when your screen locks between sets.

Macros, Meals & Full Data Portability

Log daily P/C/F macros and reusable meal presets, then export training and nutrition logs to CSV, Garmin Connect .FIT, or your own Google Sheets workbook.

Cloud Sync & Clean Profile Controls

Secured with Google Sign-In and private Firebase Cloud Firestore storage, plus centralized unit, schedule, notification, sign-out, and account deletion settings.
