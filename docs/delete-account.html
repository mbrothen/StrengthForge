Google Play Data Safety & Account Deletion

Delete Your StrengthForge Account & Data

You can permanently delete your StrengthForge account and all cloud-stored fitness and nutrition data at any time—either directly inside the Android app or by submitting a web deletion request below if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1: Delete Immediately Inside the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the StrengthForge app on your Android device and sign in.
  2. On the Dashboard tab, tap the Account / Settings icon in the top header.
  3. Tap Delete Account & All Data and confirm your Google re-authentication prompt.
  4. Your Cloud Firestore records and Firebase Authentication account are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion Online (If App Is Uninstalled)

If you have already uninstalled StrengthForge or cannot access the app, submit the request form below or email mbrothen@gmail.com from the Google Account email address associated with your StrengthForge profile.

What Data Is Deleted & Retention Timeline

  • Deleted Immediately (In-App) or Within 7 Days (Email Request): Your Firebase Authentication account (UID, email, display name), user profile preferences, all workout sessions, exercise logs, custom exercises, grouped routines, meal presets, and daily nutrition history stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Data Not Stored on Our Servers: Local CSV or Garmin .FIT files you exported to your phone's storage, and any Google Sheets spreadsheets created in your own Google Drive, stay under your direct control and can be deleted by you in your device storage or Google Drive.
  • No Residual Retention: Once your account deletion request is processed, no workout, nutrition, or personal profile records are retained.
